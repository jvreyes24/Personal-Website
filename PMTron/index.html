01 - INTRODUCTION

Project Overview

PMtron is an AI-powered project management tool designed to chat with team members concerning their workflows and tasks then provide easy to read analylitics and graphs on their progress. With PMTron, project managers do not need to individually talk with every team member and look at common roadblocks.

Role
UX/UI Designer
Tools
Figma

Goal

Optimize and simplify a project manager's role without having to constantly talk and meet with team members for consistent updates

Solution

PMtron gathers and records updates from conversations, task progress, and workflow activity and turns into clear and easily accessible information project managers can use.
